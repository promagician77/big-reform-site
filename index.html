27 offices near Dallas that could book Big Reform. Imported into HubSpot the default way, 19 of them never arrive.

Every row is a real prison, jail, juvenile facility, county office, university center or re-entry nonprofit inside the 120-mile travel range, taken from public pages on November 19, 2026. The 10 state prison units all use one web address, tdcj.texas.gov, and HubSpot matches companies on that address.

Counts come from a model of HubSpot's published import rules, run on this list: a domain repeated in one file fails as DUPLICATE_ALTERNATE_ID, and only its first row gets in. Matching on a unique buying-unit ID lets rows share a domain.

Where they are

17 of the 27 are in Dallas–Fort Worth. The farthest are about 98 miles out in a straight line, so the drive is longer. Every record carries its distance, so a list view in HubSpot can show only what fits the travel range.

Who does what

HubSpot is the record. Each of the other tools has one job and hands its result back to HubSpot.

StepToolWhat happens
FindResearch list, MarblismNew offices go into the companies file with a buying-unit ID, then into HubSpot by import. Nothing lands in HubSpot without that ID.
WriteClaude with the HubSpot connectorDrafts the first email from the company record: audience, unit type, session length. Keidrain approves it before it goes.
Send and logGmail connected to HubSpotMail goes out from Keidrain's own inbox, and sent mail and replies are logged on the contact.
Follow upHubSpotA sequence on Sales Hub Professional, or the same steps as dated tasks on Free or Starter. One owner per contact, so two tools never chase the same person.
Triage repliesMarblismSorts the inbox and drafts answers. HubSpot keeps the next follow-up.
BookHubSpot pipelinesEvent date, fee, deposit and procurement route sit on the deal, so a purchase order or a bid is tracked like a booking.

Two pipelines, because a keynote and a contract close differently

A speaking deal closes when the contract and deposit are in. An agency contract closes after vendor registration and procurement, which can take a full budget cycle. On HubSpot Free, which has no custom pipelines, both run in the default pipeline and a "Deal kind" property tells them apart.

Follow-ups counted in business days

5 touches over about 36 calendar days, then a quarterly check-in. They stop on a reply, a booked meeting, a new deal, an unsubscribe or bounce, or when the contact's next-touch owner is set to paused. Agencies close on federal holidays, so the steps skip weekends and those days. A plan written in calendar days puts 246 of 1,245 touches on a weekend or federal holiday, counted over every possible start day in 2027.
